Privacy Policy

Bharat Saathi · Effective date: 15 November 2026

Summary: Bharat Saathi keeps ordinary shop, khata, inventory and personal finance records on your device. If you choose to create an account, use linked khata, or manually make a cloud backup, the relevant information is processed by Supabase. The app currently includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK with Google test ad identifiers during development.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Bharat Saathi mobile application and its related account, linked-khata and manual cloud-backup features. The app is provided under the name Bharat Saathi (“we”, “our” or “us”).

2. Information we handle

Information stored on your device

When you use the app, you may enter shop and personal records such as customer or supplier names, sales, purchases, stock, expenses, income, credit and repayment entries, budgets, and transaction notes. These records are saved locally on your device first. While you are signed in, the app automatically synchronises a snapshot to your Supabase cloud backup when internet is available. If offline, records remain on the device and sync is retried when the app resumes or while it is running. Background sync is not guaranteed while the app is fully closed.

Account information

If you register, Supabase Auth processes your email address and password, and sends an email confirmation link. Bharat Saathi stores the name and 10-digit mobile number you provide in your profile. After email confirmation, your mobile number is used as the login identifier with your password; the app securely resolves that number to the account email on the server and does not disclose the email during mobile login. Supabase manages authentication credentials and sessions; the app does not receive or store your password in readable form.

Linked khata information

If you use the linked-khata feature, the app processes the registered mobile number used to find another account, the shop/display names, the relationship between linked accounts, and the credit/payment entries and notes that the participants create. This information is stored in the Supabase backend and is made available to the linked participants according to the feature's access rules.

Manual cloud backup

While you are signed in, the app automatically uploads a snapshot of locally stored records to your account's cloud-backup record in Supabase when internet is available. The app saves locally first and retries sync after connectivity returns or when the app resumes. You can also use the manual backup and restore controls in the app. Do not store information you do not have permission to process.

Advertising and diagnostics

The app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK for banner advertisements. The current development build uses Google's test App ID and test banner ad unit ID; these are not live monetisation identifiers. The Google Mobile Ads SDK documentation describes collection and sharing of IP address, advertising or app-set identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The exact data handled may depend on SDK version, device settings and ad configuration. Google processes this information under its own policies.

3. Why information is used

  • To provide account registration, login and password recovery.
  • To provide linked khata and display the entries shared by its participants.
  • To automatically synchronise, save and restore your cloud backup.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot and maintain the app and its backend.
  • To request and display banner ads and help prevent invalid advertising activity.

4. Service providers and sharing

  • Supabase: provides authentication, database hosting and backend services for account information, linked khata and user-requested cloud backups.
  • Google Mobile Ads: provides the banner-ad SDK and may process device and ad-related information as described above.

We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with other app users except where the linked-khata feature intentionally makes relevant entries available to the participants.

5. Storage and security

Local records remain on your device unless you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Account and cloud data are hosted by Supabase. Data transmitted to these services is sent over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. No internet-connected service can be guaranteed to be completely risk-free; protect your device and account credentials.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain account and associated server-side records while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can request deletion from the Account & Security screen inside the app. Account deletion removes the Supabase authentication account and associated account-linked server records, subject to any data that must be retained by law. The app also clears its local Bharat Saathi records on that device when you complete in-app account deletion. Data stored separately on other devices is not remotely erased by that action.

If you cannot access your account, use the external Account Deletion Request page to contact us. We may need to verify that you own the account before processing an external request.

7. Children's privacy

Bharat Saathi is a general productivity and shop-management app and is not designed for children. Do not create an account for a child or enter a child's personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Your choices

  • You can use the app's offline record-keeping features without creating an account.
  • Automatic cloud sync requires you to sign in and have an internet connection; local record keeping remains available offline.
  • You can choose not to use linked khata.
  • You can request account deletion using the in-app option or the external request page.
  • Android device settings provide controls for the advertising ID.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when a new version is published. Please review this page periodically.
