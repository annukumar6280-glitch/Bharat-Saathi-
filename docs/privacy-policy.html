Privacy Policy

Bharat Saathi · Effective date: 15 November 2026

Summary: An account is required to use Bharat Saathi. Business and personal records are saved locally first and, while signed in and online, automatically synchronized as an account-specific cloud snapshot. Local records for different signed-in accounts are kept separately on the same device. The app currently uses Google's test ad identifiers in its development build.

1. Scope and app features

This policy applies to the Bharat Saathi Android application, user accounts, linked khata, referral/commission information and automatic cloud synchronization. The current app includes personal and retailer record keeping, products and stock, purchases, sales, sales returns, customer/supplier balances and payments, expenses, income, budgets, stock adjustments, reports and CSV export. Recharge, BBPS, UPI payment/transfer and other external payment services are marked “Coming Soon” and are not active payment services.

2. Information you provide and local records

You may provide your name, email address and mobile number during registration. You may enter shop/personal khata entries, linked-khata credit/payment entries, expenses, income, budgets and transaction notes. Existing local snapshots from older versions may also contain legacy product, stock, purchase or sales records. These records are first saved in the app's private local SQLite database on your device. The app currently stores a local app snapshot; it is not a normalized server-side accounting ledger. Separate app-level encryption of the SQLite database has not been implemented.

3. Account and login information

Supabase Auth processes your registration email address and password. Your name and 10-digit mobile number are associated with your profile. After email confirmation, you can sign in using your mobile number and password. Password verification is performed by Supabase Auth; Bharat Saathi does not store your password in readable form. The phone-login service looks up the account on the server and does not return the account email to the mobile app.

4. Account separation and automatic cloud sync

Local records are stored under the identity of the signed-in account. Different accounts using the same phone have separate local snapshots and do not read or overwrite one another's account-specific records. While you are signed in and an internet connection is available, the app automatically synchronizes a snapshot to that account's cloud-backup record in Supabase. Changes are queued with a short delay; sync is retried periodically while the app is running and when it resumes. If the device is offline, records remain locally stored and are synchronized when the app gets another opportunity to run. Background sync is not guaranteed while the app is fully closed.

The latest available cloud snapshot can be restored on another device by signing in to the same account. Restore replaces that device's current local snapshot after confirmation. This is account-scoped snapshot backup, not row-by-row synchronization to a normalized accounting ledger or a conflict-safe multi-device transaction ledger. When upgrading from an older app version that used a single unassigned local snapshot, that legacy snapshot is assigned once to the first account that signs in after the upgrade. Users should sign in first with the account that previously used the device.

5. Linked khata and referral/commission information

If you use linked khata, the app processes the relevant mobile number, shop/display names, account relationship and credit/payment entries and notes created by the linked participants. This information is stored in Supabase and made available to the linked participants according to the feature's access rules. If you use referral/commission features, referral codes, eligibility and commission records may also be processed and stored in Supabase.

6. Advertising and diagnostics

The app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK banner-ad component. The current development build uses Google's test App ID and test banner ad unit ID, not live monetisation identifiers. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP address, advertising or app-set identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The exact data handled may depend on SDK version, device settings and ad configuration. Google processes this information under its own policies.

7. Purposes and service providers

  • Supabase provides authentication, database hosting and backend services for account information, linked khata, referral/commission records and automatic account-scoped cloud snapshots.
  • Google Mobile Ads provides the banner-ad SDK and may process device and ad-related information as described above.

We use information to provide login, record keeping, automatic sync/restore, linked khata, referral/commission, security, support and advertising. We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with other app users except where a feature such as linked khata intentionally makes relevant entries available to its participants.

8. Storage, security and retention

Data sent between the device and our service providers is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Local records are stored in the app's private device storage. Cloud account and associated records may be retained while your account is active or as needed to provide the service and comply with applicable law. Local data may be erased if you clear app data or uninstall the app. No internet-connected service can be guaranteed to be completely risk-free.

9. Account deletion and your choices

The current app does not contain an in-app account-deletion button. To request deletion, use the external Account Deletion Request page or contact Bharat Saathi support using the WhatsApp details below. We will verify account ownership before reviewing and processing a request. Local copies already stored on other devices may not be erased automatically. A previously signed-in session may access local records offline; an internet connection is required for a new sign-in.

10. Children's privacy

Bharat Saathi is a general productivity and shop-management app and is not designed for children. Do not create an account for a child or enter a child's personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when a new version is published. Please review this page periodically.
