Account Deletion Request

Bharat Saathi

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Bharat Saathi and go to Profile.
  2. Open “Account & Security”.
  3. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  4. Choose “Delete Account” and confirm the permanent deletion.

In-app deletion removes the account from Supabase Auth and deletes associated account-linked server records. The app also clears Bharat Saathi's local records on that device. Local records on other devices are not remotely erased.

Cannot sign in?

Contact support from the registered email address and request deletion of your Bharat Saathi account. We will verify account ownership before processing the request. Do not send your password or one-time password.

Request deletion via WhatsApp

The request must include the email address registered to the account. If you cannot use WhatsApp, use the Help Centre contact shown in the app.

What is deleted?

  • Your Supabase authentication account and login sessions.
  • Your account profile, linked-khata associations and transactions associated with your account.
  • Your server-side cloud backup and account-linked records that are configured to be deleted with the account.
  • Local Bharat Saathi records on the device where you complete in-app deletion.

Information that must be retained to meet legal obligations may be retained for the legally required period. Deletion on one device does not erase data independently stored on another device.
