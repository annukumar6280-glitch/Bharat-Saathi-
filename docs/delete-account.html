Account Deletion Request

Bharat Saathi

How to request deletion

The current Bharat Saathi app does not have an in-app account-deletion button. You can request deletion using the support link below. We will verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Request deletion via WhatsApp

Include the email address and mobile number registered to your Bharat Saathi account. Do not send your password or OTP. If you cannot use WhatsApp, contact support from the registered email address.

What happens after verification?

After verifying account ownership, support will review the request and process deletion of the Supabase authentication account and associated server-side records that belong to that account, subject to applicable legal retention requirements. We will not ask you to share your password or OTP.

What is not automatically removed?

  • Local records on the device where the app was installed may remain until you clear the app data or uninstall it.
  • Local records or exports on other devices are not remotely erased by this request.
  • Records that must be retained to meet legal obligations may be retained for the legally required period.
